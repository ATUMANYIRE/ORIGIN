# ORIGIN — Digital Twin Architecture (`Milestone 03/05`)

## 1. Purpose & Epistemic Discipline
The ORIGIN **Living Digital Twin** provides an executable, versioned computational representation of a distributed software system. Every Digital Twin model and simulation branch obeys strict epistemic classification:

- `historical_observation` — Direct OpenTelemetry traces (`span_observations`) and metrics (`metric_observations`).
- `experimental_evidence` — Controlled A/B intervention trials (`experiment_runs`, `evidence_records`).
- `calibrated_model` — Versioned Digital Twin parameters estimated from a training split and evaluated against a held-out validation split (`digital_twin_models`, `calibration_runs`).
- `counterfactual_prediction` — Simulated trajectories and delta comparisons generated by the Digital Twin (`simulation_branches`, `simulation_snapshots`).

Simulated trajectories never overwrite or mutate historical evidence records.

## 2. Ten-Layer Separation of Concerns
The Digital Twin subsystem (`backend/app/modules/simulation/`) separates 10 computational responsibilities across [`simulator.py`](../backend/app/modules/simulation/simulator.py), [`calibration.py`](../backend/app/modules/simulation/calibration.py), [`service.py`](../backend/app/modules/simulation/service.py), and [`schemas.py`](../backend/app/modules/simulation/schemas.py):

1. **Model Definitions**: Service capacity (`processing_capacity_rps`), replica count (`replica_count`), concurrency limits (`concurrency_limit`), queue bounds (`max_queue_depth`), base latency (`base_latency_ms`), shared DB connection capacity (`db_connection_capacity`), and circuit-breaker thresholds (`circuit_breaker_threshold_ms`).
2. **State Transitions**: Bottom-up topological evaluation (`order-database-sim → payment-simulator → inventory-service → checkout-service → api-gateway`) computing downstream backpressure, row-lock contention, queue accumulation, Little's Law active concurrency, saturation, and error propagation.
3. **Simulation Clock**: Explicit floating-point clock (`sim_clock_sec`) advancing by deterministic time steps (`dt_seconds`).
4. **Event Scheduler**: Priority-queue scheduler (`EventScheduler`) backed by `heapq` with monotonic sequence numbers `(timestamp_sec, seq, event)`.
5. **Evidence-Based Calibration**: `TwinCalibrationEngine` decomposing intrinsic vs. child span latencies on a 75% training split and scoring residuals (`MAE`, `RMSE`, `NRMSE`) on a 25% held-out validation split.
6. **Intervention Application**: Allowlisted counterfactual perturbations applied at scheduled simulation timestamps (`apply_at_sec`).
7. **State Snapshots**: Full serialization of service states, active interventions, pending event priority queue, and Python `random.Random` state (`_rng_state_to_json`).
8. **Branch Management**: Isolated baseline and counterfactual execution branches (`simulation_branches`) with resource caps (`MAX_HORIZON_SECONDS = 300`, `MAX_EVENTS_PER_RUN = 5000`, `MAX_BRANCHES_PER_TWIN = 60`).
9. **Outcome Evaluation**: Summary metrics (`mean_latency_ms`, `p95_latency_ms`, `mean_error_rate_pct`, `mean_throughput_rps`, `max_queue_depth`, `recovery_time_sec`, `primary_bottleneck_service`).
10. **Uncertainty Estimation**: Seeded Monte Carlo ensemble execution (`run_simulation_with_uncertainty`) producing 95% prediction intervals per time step and across branch summaries.
